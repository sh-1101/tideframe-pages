Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hiroki Sugiyama ("we") handles information in the iOS app Tideframe (the "App"). The App creates an image by overlaying information such as species, date and tide curves on your fishing photos. If this English version differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese version prevails.

Information we collect

The App does not collect personal information such as your name or email address. There are no accounts and we operate no servers. Photos are processed on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. We may receive only the purchase information described under "Purchases" below, statistics Apple provides to developers (such as crash information if you agreed to share it), and your email address and message if you contact us. Inquiry information is used only to respond to you.

Photos and works

A photo you choose is copied to the App's storage on your device so that you can edit it again. The copy is a newly encoded image without metadata such as the capture location. The original photo is not changed. The details you enter (species, size, date, method, lure, tackle, area), the photo's capture date and your display settings are stored in the same place. None of this is sent to us or anyone else. However, this storage is included in iCloud or device backups if you have them enabled. Images are saved to Photos or shared only when you choose to do so.

Photo location

The App does not access your device's current location. If a photo contains a capture location (EXIF GPS data), it is used only to choose the nearest tide station.

For this purpose, the latitude and longitude are used only in your device's memory while the App is running. They are not included in works (including the photo copy), exported images or logs. A saved work does, however, contain the selected tide station name.

The tide station shown on an exported image, or what appears in the photo itself, may let others infer where it was taken. Please check the image before sharing it.

Network connections

The App connects from your device to the providers below to obtain data. It does not send your photos, work contents or latitude and longitude. Information required for communication, such as your IP address, reaches the provider.

  • Japan Meteorological Agency (tide data): data is downloaded per station, for a year or a month at a time. If you choose a photo that has a capture location and a capture date with time zone information, data for the nearest station within 50 km is downloaded automatically. The provider can see which station was requested (the same applies when you choose a station yourself). Downloaded data is kept temporarily on your device.

Purchases

Payment for the "Tideframe Pro" subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store. To check and manage purchase status, the App uses the purchase management service RevenueCat, which processes an automatically generated anonymous identifier, your purchase history, basic device and app information (such as OS version, app version, locale and currency), and the IP address used for communication. RevenueCat is a U.S. company, and this information may be processed outside Japan. We may view anonymous purchase information in RevenueCat's dashboard to understand purchase activity. We never receive your name, email address or card details.

Analytics and advertising

The App contains no analytics service, shows no ads and does not track you for advertising.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a work by long-pressing it on the home screen. Deleting the App erases works and App data on your device, although existing iCloud or device backups may still contain them. Images saved to Photos and your original photos are not deleted. To request deletion of anonymous purchase information held by RevenueCat, contact us below with information that identifies the purchase (such as the order ID on your Apple receipt). We may be unable to act on a request if the purchase cannot be identified.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy as needed. The latest version is always posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
